2. Data we do NOT collect

  • No location, no contacts, no photos, no camera or microphone data — the app asks for none of these permissions.
  • No IMEI or SIM number. On Android 10+ an app cannot read IMEI; the app instead uses its own app-generated deviceId (see above).
  • No advertising identifier and no third-party advertising SDK was found in the project. No data is sold to anyone.

3. How data is used and shared

  • Your data is used only to run the app, manage your trial/licence, enforce the device-binding rule, and answer support requests.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, App Check) to operate the service. Google processes it under its own terms as our service provider.
  • Your user record can be read by the app's admin/support roles only for licence approval and support — that is how trial re-approval works. It is not published, sold, or shared with advertisers.
  • No data is shared with any other third party except as required by law.

4. Security

Access to user records is controlled by Firestore security rules (role-based; only admin/support can read user records, and no user can self-extend a trial, self-grant premium, or start a second trial). Sign-in uses Google's authentication; keep your Google account secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Your account record is kept while your account exists, so your trial/licence and device bindings can be checked on sign-in.

To ask for deletion of your account data, contact support at zakootaapps@gmail.com or WhatsApp +923336638888 from the email / number on the account. Deletion removes your user record; a backup or log copy may persist for a short time before it is overwritten.

Deleting the app from your device alone does not delete the server-side account record — please ask support to delete it. See also Account Deletion Request.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child's data was provided, contact support and it will be deleted.
